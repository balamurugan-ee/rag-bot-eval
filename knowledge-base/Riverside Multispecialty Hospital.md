# Appointments

Outpatient consultations require an appointment.

### Appointment Contact

**Phone:** +91 44 4100 2200
**Location:** Main Reception

### Appointment Information

Patients may provide:

* Patient name
* Preferred department
* Preferred date
* Preferred time
* Contact number

### Cancellation

Appointments should be cancelled at least **2 hours before the scheduled consultation**.

### Late Arrival

Patients arriving more than **20 minutes late** may need to reschedule.

### Walk-ins

Walk-in outpatient consultations are not guaranteed.
